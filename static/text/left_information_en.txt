Project name
Liquid fertilizer treatment system for communal resource recovery facilities capable of processing 30 m³/day

Background
The background of the project stems from increasing environmental concerns related to livestock manure management, including greenhouse gas emissions from manure decomposition and stricter national regulations governing livestock waste treatment and resource recycling. Current treatment systems often suffer from operational inefficiency and complex management requirements, highlighting the need for a more efficient and intelligent solution. 

Location
Gunwi Livestock Cooperative Natural Circular Agriculture Center, Daegu
