Time Interval Options

Default graph represent all data up to current time Today with 5 minutes interval. Time Interval Buttons support to manipulate the data in the graphs. There are 5 buttons to manipulate time interval. When the different buttons are selected, the time interval values on X-axis of the graph will change and the green line will present the values at specific time interval.

  • 30 minutes: It means 30 minutes interval. The values at every 30 minutes starting from 00:00 hour or starting time to collect data on each day are considered to display in the graph. (eg. 00:00, 00:30, 01:00, etc or 00:05, 00:035, 01:05, etc))
  • One hour: It means one hour interval. The values at every one hour starting from 00:00 hour or starting time to collect data on each day are considered to display in the graph. (eg. 00:00, 01:00, 02:00, etc or (00:05, 01:05, 02:05, etc)
  • One day: It means one day interval. The values at every one day (24 hours) starting from 00:00 hour on each day are considered to display in the graph. (eg. 2025/10/11 00:00, 2025/10/12 00:00, 2025/10/13 00:00, etc or 2025/10/11 02:00, 2025/10/12 02:00, 2025/10/13 02:00, etc)
  • One week: It means one week interval. The values at every seven days starting from 00:00 hour on each day are considered to display in the graph.
  • One month: It means one month interval. The values at every one month (30 days) starting from 00:00 hour on each day are considered to display in the graph.
